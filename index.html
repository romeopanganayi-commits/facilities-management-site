Why this course

Get the best out of your facilities – within budget, safely and for their whole lifetime.

Facilities management keeps buildings, plant, utilities and services working. Done well, it balances functionality, availability, reliability and safety against cost over the full life of each asset.

This course shows you how to establish, run and continually improve your facilities department: plan and budget properly, build a risk-based maintenance strategy, decide what to outsource, manage contractors and measure performance so you can prove the value your team delivers.
