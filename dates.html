Booking terms in brief

Simple, clear terms

Payment

Fees are payable on booking unless an approved purchase order is provided. ICL invoices in South African Rand (ZAR) or US Dollars (USD).

Cancellations

Cancel in writing at least 14 days before the course for a refund or credit note. Later cancellations carry a 25% administration fee.

Substitutions

Can’t attend? You may send a colleague in your place or move to a later session of the same course.
